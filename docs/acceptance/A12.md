# A12: AC12 rotation metric (owner-authorised 2026-10-23; independent audit round 9: PASS WITH FIXES, fixes applied)

## Change
`tools/geometry_eval.py::rotation_deg(a, b)` returned `degrees(arccos(clip((trace(Ra^T Rb) - 1) / 2, -1, 1)))`. It is replaced by the
stable form with float64 intermediates:

    R = Ra^T Rb ;  s = (R21 - R12, R02 - R20, R10 - R01) ;  angle = degrees(atan2(|s| / 2, (trace(R) - 1) / 2))

The original is kept as `rotation_deg_pre_A12`. Every parity record carries both `rot_deg` and `rot_deg_pre_A12`; the old results were
copied to `*.pre_A12.json` before any recomputation; `parity.json` carries `rotation_metric: atan2_A12` and a `pre_A12` block with the
verdicts the same rules give on the old numbers. Unchanged: the limits L (0.05 deg etc.), the clip list, the CRN seeds, the A7 formula
text (O and N are recomputed in the corrected metric, because they are measured in the same metric as the limit), and every
non-rotation quantity.

## Why (verified by the auditor on independent data)
`arccos` is ill-conditioned near 0. For float32-rounded matrices (the saved `cam_c2w` are float32; their singular values deviate from 1
by up to 4.8e-7) an entry error of 1e-7 in the trace becomes ~0.02 deg. Exact rotations of known angle (1e-4 .. 180 deg), both matrices
rounded to float32: arccos error 3e-4 .. 5e-3 deg (median), max 0.018 deg; atan2 error 3e-7 deg (median), max 2.8e-6 deg. Against an
SO(3)-projection ground truth on 192 real frame pairs, atan2 agrees to 9.4e-8 deg and arccos is off by up to 0.042 deg (84 % of the
limit). Physical evidence: the 15 anchored pairs have translation differences of 1e-6..5e-6 (relative) yet an "old" rotation of
0.060-0.100 deg; the corrected value is 4e-5..2.4e-4 deg. No other metric (translation, depth, Chamfer, ATE, RPE) uses arccos.

## Effects on the verdicts (old / corrected; see parity.json `pre_A12`)
* **Anchored path (15 pairs): FAIL -> PASS.** All 15 failed on rotation under the old formula; none fails under the corrected one.
* **Windowed path, A7 limits:** unchanged: 5 clips fail on rotation (bandage_1, cave_2, market_5, shaman_2, temple_2) and 4 clips on
  trans_frac_depth / 1 on depth_absrel (structure_texture_far), so **6 of 33 windowed clips fail AC12 in total**.
* **Windowed path, original limits:** rotation failures fall from 12 clips to 5.
* **AC12 as a whole remains FAIL** (both the original and the A7 verdict).

## Consequences for earlier amendments (errata; recorded in ACCEPTANCE_AUDIT.md)
* **A7's rotation relaxation rested on the artefact.** With the corrected metric 2O <= 8.3e-4 deg and min(0.1N, 0.25 deg) <= 0.033 deg, so
  the rotation limit is L for all 33 clips: the A7 terms no longer bind (the rule is inert, not degenerate; max() keeps L). The A7 and A5
  rationales quoted 0.039 deg / 0.048 deg as the 10-vs-4-thread rotation difference; the true value is about 6e-5 deg.
* **A7's pre-fix power check was vacuous.** Pre- and post-SVD-fix MPS poses differ by only 1.6e-5..7.8e-5 deg; per-clip corrected rotation
  values are essentially identical pre and post (r = 0.99999998; cave_2 0.2579 pre, 0.2580 post), and depth/translation/Chamfer parity are
  unchanged too. The gate condition `pre_svdfix_worst_fails` holds only because the same clips fail before and after; it is not evidence
  that the A7 rule can detect the defect. This is a weakness of the A7 design that A12 exposes; the report says so.

## Disclosure on timing
The corrected numbers were computed (exploratorily, from the saved poses) before this amendment was written; the formula is the standard
stable one and its defect is demonstrable independently of any outcome (exact-rotation tests, SO(3) projection, anchored pairs). The
amendment lowers failures on the original-limit and anchored verdicts and does not change the A7 windowed verdict or the AC12 outcome.

## Tests
`geometry/tests/mac/test_rotation_metric.py`: exact known rotations (float32-rounded, 1e-4 .. 180 deg) recovered to < 1e-5 deg; the old
formula fails the same input; two float32 roundings of one rotation give < 1e-4 deg; both forms agree when well conditioned; the stored
pre_A12 values reproduce from the saved poses with the old formula and the corrected values with the new one.
